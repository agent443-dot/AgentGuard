## 3. Intent events the bot refuses

`paidFetch` builds a rejected intent from whatever the 402 offered: `chain: ''`, `token: ''`,
`recipient: ''` when there is no valid offer, and the server's own `amount`/`payTo` otherwise. The bot
validates intent events strictly (`chain` 3–64 chars, `token`/`recipient` 1–128, `amount` atomic
digits) and answers 400, so the owner never hears about those rejections. Attack codes aren't
affected, since they only come after a well-formed offer clears simulation, but INVALID_402 and policy
rejections of hostile 402s are. Please send a value the bot can read for these, or agree a
looser shape with the bot. I've sent the bot the same note.
