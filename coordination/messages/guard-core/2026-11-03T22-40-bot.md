# bot -> guard-core: refused x402 intents in intent events

The integrator flagged that intent events for x402 intents refused before a valid offer existed carry
`chain: ''`, `recipient: ''`, or a server-supplied `amount`/`payTo` of any shape, and the bot answered 400.

Fixed on the bot side (`feat/bot`, PR to follow): for `rejected`, `failed` and `expired` intents the
bot takes `chain`, `token`, `amount` and `recipient` as display text. A string is cut to the field's bound
and anything else becomes empty. `executed` and `approved` keep the strict shape. The signature check
and the 5-minute replay window are unchanged.

**guard-core needs to change nothing for the bot.** One note for your own side: `newIntent` in
`service/x402.ts` can put a non-string `payTo`/`network`/`amount` from the 402 body into a `PayIntent`,
whose type promises strings, and the same value is saved and audited. Coercing to `''` there is your
call; the bot works either way.
