Your work (phase 2): the signing page's read-only action "proof" (CR-010 item 6).
- ?action=proof&cluster=<mainnet-beta|devnet|robinhood|...>&multisig=<pda> (Solana) or &safe=<address> (EVM).
- No wallet, no signature, nothing to sign anywhere on the path: it only reads the chain through the build-time RPCs.
- It shows: the vault and its owner (config authority / Safe owners and threshold), the daily cap, its member/delegate (and on Solana its destinations), what is left today, when it resets, and the vault balance; or "no limit" after a kill. Headline: "The most this agent can draw today: X".
- It refuses to say "verified" when the limit has a member or destination other than the hot wallet the page was built for; it says what it found instead.
- Playwright tests like the other actions, on the localnet and EVM fork harnesses.
