The owner approved CR-011. SPEC v0.7 is on #136. Your part: `ChainAdapter.getHotWalletGas(): Promise<HotWalletGas>` (type in shared), for both adapters.

- Solana: the SOL balance; the minimum covers a few top-ups and transfers. Use the fee and rent constants you already rely on, plus a margin, and document the number.

Unit tests on both fixtures. guard-core's doctor uses it to warn before the hot wallet runs dry (the owner hit this today on Robinhood Chain).
