## The idea: a boot sequence

The whole video is the AgentGuard system **booting up on screen**, told by AgentGuard itself in the
first person. It opens inside the robot's eyes (a HUD about to pay), the Guard cuts in, then five
**shields** power on one by one, then an **install sequence** of four moves. Every section starts with
a HUD line that types itself (`SHIELD 01 // ONLINE`), so the viewer always knows where they are and
waits for the next one.

Metaphors: the AI agent is a **robot helper**; the vault is a **piggy bank**; the onchain daily cap is
a **lock that lets out only a little each day**; the simulation is a **practice world**; `/kill` is
**one button**. Each maps to a real feature (fact check at the end). Energetic, never hype: no
"unhackable", no promises of gains, nothing about any token.

## Voices

- **GUARD** (almost every line): deep, calm, warm, cinematic. Confident, never shouting.
- **ROBOT** (two short lines, scene 1): lighter, slightly synthetic. If you use one voice only, read
  them with a robotic filter in the edit, or let GUARD read them.
- `…` is a breath; `[beat]` is about one second of silence (`<break time="1s"/>` where supported).
  Delete the marks if your tool reads them aloud.
- Spelled for text-to-speech: "slash kill", "M C P".

## Script to paste — English

```
ROBOT: Task: buy weather data. Price: fifty cents. Wallet… connected.

GUARD: Stop. [beat] This is the moment everything can go wrong.

Your AI agent is a robot helper. It works all day, all night, faster than you can blink. And now… it can pay.

But if the robot holds the key to your piggy bank, one bad instruction, one trick, one bug… and the whole piggy bank is open.

That's the problem. [beat] I'm the answer.

I'm AgentGuard. I stand between your robot and your money.

The robot never gets the key. It can only ask me. And every time it asks… five shields switch on.

Shield one. Your rules. How much per payment. How much per day. Which websites. Anything else? No.

Shield two. A practice run. Before any money moves, I play the payment in a practice world, to see what it would really do. Not what the robot says it does.

Shield three. Your yes. A big payment? Your phone lights up on Telegram. You say yes, or no. If the price suddenly jumps, or the payee changes, I ask you too.

Shield four. The lock. Your money sits in your own vault, and only your Phantom wallet controls it. A lock built into the blockchain lets out only a little each day. Even if someone breaks me… the lock holds.

Shield five. The button. Something feels wrong? Slash kill. Every robot freezes. One signature in Phantom… and the door is shut.

[beat] Now. How do you use me? Four moves.

Move one. Run the setup wizard. It makes your keys on your own machine, builds your guard, and hands you the links to sign in Phantom. Your guard. Your vault. No AgentGuard service in the middle.

Move two. Connect your robot. One setup command, for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any M C P agent.

Move three. Watch it in shadow mode. Your robot tries to pay… and I sign nothing. I just show you what it would have spent.

Move four. Happy? Pick your limits in Telegram, allow the sites you trust, and switch to live. Then check your report.

Got a big job? Give the robot a budget for that one task, with its own limit and an expiry. Paying too fast? A breaker trips.

Live today, on Robinhood Chain and Solana. Base is next.

I'm new, and I'm open source. Every line of code, and every known risk, is out in the open.

[beat] Robots are getting wallets. Give them pocket money. Not your keys.

AgentGuard. [beat] Boot yours.
```

About 420 words, about 2:40 at a cinematic pace.
